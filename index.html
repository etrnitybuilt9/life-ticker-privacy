2. What Life Ticker does

Life Ticker is a glanceable information app. It can show:

  • headlines and short publisher-provided excerpts from public news feeds;
  • current weather from Open-Meteo;
  • the next event from your device calendar, if you choose to enable it;
  • a personal sticky note.

These can appear in the in-app feed, a home-screen widget and an optional overlay rail above other apps. Calendar titles and times can appear on the widget and overlay only after the in-app disclosure and only when the Calendar ticker is enabled.

The beta has no user accounts, no advertising, no subscriptions, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no AI processing and no GPS or device-location permission.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally in Android app storage:

  • your selected topics and publisher choices;
  • the place you enter for weather, the resolved place and coordinates, and the latest weather cache;
  • cached headline titles, article links, publisher-provided excerpts, publication times and fetch times;
  • your sticky note and whether it is shown in the ticker;
  • display preferences, widget and overlay item state, snooze state and recent item IDs.

This information stays in the app's local storage unless a section below says it is sent externally.

A development build can be installed at the same time as the beta. The two installs have separate app data:

  • Life Ticker Beta uses application ID com.lifeticker.beta.
  • Life Ticker uses application ID com.lifeticker.

This policy applies only to the beta install. Check Display → Help & about to confirm which installation you are using.

4. Network requests and external providers

News feeds

The app requests headlines from the publisher feeds enabled in the app. Recommended feeds are enabled by default for your selected topics; you can change them under Content → News sources. News requests include standard request data such as your IP address and a basic LifeTicker/1.0 User-Agent. The app receives headline metadata, article links and publisher-provided excerpts. It does not download full article pages, bypass paywalls or store full article text. Disabling a publisher prevents further requests for that publisher's feed.

Publisher content remains the property of its publisher. Cached headlines, links and excerpts remain local to the installed app.

Sources in the internal-development build

The current internal-development build contains these feeds for development and quality assurance only:

  • BBC World: feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/world/rss.xml
  • The Verge: theverge.com/rss/index.xml
  • Guardian Australia Business: theguardian.com/australia-news/business-australia/rss
  • Guardian Australia: theguardian.com/australia-news/rss

These sources have not been cleared for external beta distribution. Their presence in the development build is not a statement that redistribution permission has been granted.

Publishers approved for an external beta

As of the effective date, no publisher has been approved and enabled for an externally distributed beta. This section will list each approved publisher and its feed address before any external beta build is provided to testers. The approved beta catalogue may differ from the internal-development list above.

Choosing Read full article or Open in Calendar leaves the app. Articles open in your browser, normally through Android Custom Tabs, and the destination website's terms and privacy policy apply. Calendar events open in your calendar app.

Weather

Weather uses Open-Meteo:

  • when the app needs to resolve a new place, the place you enter is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service;
  • the resolved latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo's forecast service;
  • the app receives current and daily weather values, which are cached locally.

If the entered place has not changed, the app reuses the saved coordinates and does not geocode the place again. Open-Meteo receives standard request data such as your IP address. No GPS or device-location permission is used.

Open-Meteo's terms state that the free API is for non-commercial use and that its log files may contain IP addresses and geographic coordinates, with logs deleted after 90 days. See Open-Meteo terms and Open-Meteo licence. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo.com under the CC BY 4.0 licence.

No developer backend

Life Ticker has no developer-operated account system, application server or database. Apart from the publisher feeds and Open-Meteo requests described above, the app does not send your local data to the developer.

Support email

Choosing Email beta support opens your preferred email app with the approved address. The app does not automatically attach diagnostics, logs, headline data, calendar data or sticky-note content. You choose what to send.

5. Calendar

Calendar access is optional. If you grant read-calendar permission:

  • the app queries events in the next seven days and uses the first eligible event;
  • it reads the event ID, title, start and end time, all-day flag and cancellation status;
  • it does not edit the calendar or read descriptions, attendees, locations, links or notes;
  • event information is not sent to the developer or to a network service;
  • if the Calendar ticker is on, the event title and time can appear in the in-app feed, the home-screen widget and the optional overlay above other apps.

You can revoke calendar permission in Android settings at any time. Calendar visibility and the Android permission are separate controls.

6. Widget, overlay and notifications

The optional overlay rail requires Android's Display over other apps permission. It displays ticker content above other apps but does not read the screen, control other apps or collect what you type.

The rail runs a foreground service with a generic ongoing notification. The notification does not contain headline, calendar, sticky-note or other ticker text. Notification permission is optional; the rail can run without it, though its foreground notification may be hidden.

The optional Quick Settings tile starts, snoozes or resumes the rail. It does not collect additional data.

The home-screen widget displays the current ticker item on your home screen. Anyone who can see the screen can see it.

7. Backup and device transfer

Life Ticker disables Android's standard app-data cloud backup and device-to-device transfer mechanisms through its application configuration (android:allowBackup="false" and exclusion rules for cloud backup and device transfer). This does not control independent backups, screenshots, screen recordings or other copies created outside the app. Local app data is not copied to another device by those Android mechanisms.

Ordinary app updates over an installed beta build do not clear local preferences. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does. Deleting local app data is not reversible through Life Ticker's disabled Android backup configuration. Updating from a build with a different package name or signing key requires uninstalling first, which removes local data.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Preferences, sticky note, weather cache and headline cache remain until you change or clear them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.
  • The headline cache can include items from disabled or temporarily failing publishers. There is no age-based expiry in this build.
  • There is no developer-side account or server database for this app.
  • Publisher and Open-Meteo requests are handled under their own retention policies.

The beta and development installs have separate app data. Identify the correct installation before deleting anything:

  • Life Ticker Beta — application ID com.lifeticker.beta.
  • Life Ticker — application ID com.lifeticker.

You can remove beta local data at any time:

  1. Android Settings → Apps → Life Ticker Beta → Storage & cache → Clear storage; or
  2. uninstall the Life Ticker Beta app.

Check Display → Help & about to confirm the application ID before clearing data or uninstalling. Clearing the development install affects only the development app's separate data. You can also revoke Calendar, Display over other apps and Notification permissions in Android settings. There is no account or server-side profile to delete.

Clearing app data or uninstalling is not reversible through Life Ticker's disabled Android backup configuration.

If you are in the private beta, email etrnitybuilt9@gmail.com to ask to be removed from the tester list. That does not itself delete data on your device.

9. Analytics, crash reporting and diagnostics

There are no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or telemetry SDKs in this beta. No data is sold, and the app does not create an account or profile.

The app does not transmit diagnostics automatically. If you email support, the app does not attach logs or device data; you decide what to include. If a future distribution method adds Google Play vitals or a crash-reporting service, this policy will be updated before that build is distributed.
